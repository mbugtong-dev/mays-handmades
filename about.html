Tambayan for TUPians

Welcome to Tambayan for TUPians! This guide is designed to help you make the most of your time at TUP by providing useful information and resources. This guide is created from the author's experience as a 1st year student who had long vacant hours that seeked for tambayan where she can relax as well as do her activities. These places are near TUP and is also in a walkable distance.
